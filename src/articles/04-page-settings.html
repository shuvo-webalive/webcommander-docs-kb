The Page settings API lets your software read and change what a store's category pages, product pages, search result page, shopping cart and checkout page show, and create, change and delete the product promotions on the search result page. All thirty-one endpoints are available in all seven WebCommander SDKs.

Each page has its own reference: Category page, Product page, Search result page, Shopping cart and Checkout page. A page's settings are split into sections, and you can read or change the whole page at once or one section at a time.

Common tasks

You want to Use
Show the category description on category pages Update a settings section on the category page, with category_information as the section and show_description: true
Change which buttons and details product pages show Get a product page section to see the current values, then Update a product page section with configuration as the section and only the fields that change
Promote products at the top of search results Create a product promotion with a name, rule: "all_search_result", position: "top" and the products' ids in product_ids
Pause a promotion without deleting it Turn a product promotion on or off with is_active: false, passing the promotion's id as promotion_id
Turn on the slide-out flying cart Update a shopping cart section with flying_cart as the section and enabled: true
Let customers check out without an account Update a settings section on the checkout page, with customer_checkout as the section and enabled: true inside checkout_as_guest

All endpoints

Category page

Use these endpoints to choose how category pages show the category and its subcategories (the category_information section) and its products (the product_information section). For more detail, see the Category page overview.

{{endpoints:category_page}}

Product page

Use these endpoints to choose what product pages show, from action buttons and variations to images, up-sells, related products, reviews and quick view, in seven sections such as configuration and quick_view. For more detail, see the Product page overview.

{{endpoints:product_page}}

Search result page

Use these endpoints to choose how the search result page shows products (the product_information section), and to list, create, change, turn on or off and delete the product promotions shown on it. For more detail, see the Search result page overview.

{{endpoints:search_result_page}}

Shopping cart

Use these endpoints to choose what the cart page (the cart_page section) and the slide-out flying cart (the flying_cart section) offer shoppers. For more detail, see the Shopping cart overview.

{{endpoints:shopping_cart}}

Checkout page

Use these endpoints to read the checkout page's billing and shipping address forms (the billing_address and shipping_address sections), and to change its sign-in, delivery, order summary and other settings (the customer_checkout, delivery_options, order_summary and others sections). For more detail, see the Checkout page overview.

Good to know

Wrap every write in the right key

On a whole page's path, such as /category_page, wrap the fields in the page's key and then the section name: {"category_page": {"category_information": {...}}}. On a section's path, such as /category_page/category_information, wrap them in the section's own name: {"category_information": {...}}. On the category page, shopping cart and checkout page, a section write wrapped in the page's key, or not wrapped at all, is rejected with 400 invalid_request. Promotion bodies are wrapped in product_promotion.

After a rejected call, the next call can go wrong too

On the checkout page, the call after a 400 on the same client gets the previous call's response instead of its own, and a write in it is not saved. On the shopping cart, the write after a rejected write on the same client returns the same rejection again, even when it is correct. In both cases, the call after that works normally. On the checkout page, make your next call from a new client after a 400.

Ask for a description of every field

Add field_metadata=true to any settings read to also get a description of every field. The settings are still returned with it. On the product page it shows which fields depend on another in depends_on. On the checkout page's customer_checkout and delivery_options sections, it lists the rules a write must follow in _rules.

  • Send only the fields you change. PATCH is the only way to change settings, and fields you leave out keep their stored values. On the product page, sending a section back exactly as you read it can be rejected with 400 and the code constraint_violation, because a field cannot be set to true while a field it depends on is off.
  • Product page and search result page sections come back inside the page's key. Reading the product page's quick_view section returns {"product_page": {"quick_view": {...}}}, not {"quick_view": {...}}. The category page, shopping cart and checkout page return a section under its own name.
  • Some checkout settings are read-only or cannot all be off. The billing_address and shipping_address sections are read-only. In customer_checkout, at least one of allow_sign_in, allow_sign_up and checkout_as_guest must stay on, and in delivery_options, at least one of the four delivery options must stay on. A write that breaks this is rejected with 400 and the code constraint_violation.
  • On the search result page, only product_information can be changed. A write to seo or reinitialise is rejected with 400. The promotions are not in the settings: use List product promotions. It ignores limit, so send offset to skip promotions. The store holds a limited number of promotions, which the listing gives as max_items when you add field_metadata=true, so delete promotions you no longer need.
  • Writes are rate limited. Writes to a page and to all its sections share one limit. Once it is used up, the next write is rejected with 429 and a Retry-After header, and writes to the page and its sections are rejected until that time has passed, so wait before you write again. On the category page, product page, shopping cart and checkout page, the limit is ten writes in a short time, and on the checkout page it also blocks writes to the product page settings. Promotion writes have their own limit. The product page and checkout page also limit reads: the twenty-first read in about one and a half seconds on the product page, or in about one second on the checkout page, is rejected with 429.

The Customer groups API lets your software work with a store's customer groups. You can list, count, create, retrieve, update and delete groups, and choose which customers belong to each group. Each group also holds its own purchase, tax and product display settings, under purchase_preference, tax_configuration and product_display. All nine endpoints are available in all seven WebCommander SDKs.

Common tasks

You want to Use
Find groups by name, or only the active ones, or count them List customer groups with search_text, such as search_text=wholesale, and status=active or status=inactive. It returns the groups whose name contains that text, in any letter case. Count customer groups takes the same filters and returns the number of matches as total
Add a group, such as one for trade accounts Create a customer group with {"customer_group": {"name": "Wholesale Partners"}}. Only name is required. It returns the new group, with the group's id, which the other calls take as customer_group_id
Change one setting of a group, such as making it tax exempt Update a customer group with {"customer_group": {"tax_configuration": {"tax_exempt": true}}}. Only the fields you send change, and it returns the updated group
Add one customer to a group and keep its other members Add a customer to a customer group with the customer's customer_id as customer and the group's customer_group_id as group: {"customer": 5678, "group": 1234}. It returns 201 whether or not it added the customer: added is true when the customer joined and false when the customer was already a member
See who is in a group, or check whether one customer is Retrieve a customer group returns its members under customers and their number as customer_count. Retrieve a member of a customer group with the group's customer_group_id and the customer's customer_id returns that member, or 404 when that customer is not a member
Set exactly who is in a group, or take a customer out of it Replace the members of a customer group with the customer_id of every customer who should be in the group, such as {"customers": [5678, 5679]}. A customer you leave out leaves the group. It returns the group with its new members
Remove a group Delete a customer group. It deletes the group permanently and returns 204 with no body. Its members stay in the store as customers

These endpoints list, count, create, retrieve, update and delete customer groups, and replace, check and add their members. The Customer groups overview lists what each one does.

Good to know

Replacing a group's members removes every customer you leave out of the list. Replace the members of a customer group makes the list you send the whole membership, and {"customers": []} empties the group. To add one customer and keep the others, use Add a customer to a customer group instead.

Wrap a group in customer_group when you create or update it. Send {"customer_group": {"name": "Wholesale Partners"}}. A create or update without the wrapper is rejected with 400 and the message customer_group is required. Replacing the members and adding a customer take no wrapper: send {"customers": [...]} to replace the members, and {"customer": ..., "group": ...} to add a customer. A replace that sends a bare list instead of {"customers": [...]} returns 500 with the message Unexpected Error Occurred; wrap the list in customers.

An update changes only the fields you send, inside nested settings too. {"customer_group": {"tax_configuration": {"tax_exempt": true}}} changes tax_exempt and keeps the other five tax settings. There is no replace: PUT on a group is rejected with 405.

  • Members are customers, identified by their customer_id. The member calls take the customer's customer_id, as a number or a string, not the customer's internal_id. In a group's customers list, each member's id is that customer's customer_id. To see which groups a customer is in, Retrieve a customer and read customer_groups.
  • A listing returns 20 groups per call unless you send limit. limit takes 1 to 100. To get more, send a higher offset, or the next page, while pagination.has_next is true. To sort by date, send sort=created or sort=updated; sort=created_at and sort=updated_at are rejected with 400.
  • There is no HEAD call. HEAD returns 404 on every customer group path, even though the Allow header of an OPTIONS request lists it on the listing, count and group paths. Use GET instead.
  • Groups already in the store can hold values a write rejects. A stored group can have a status other than A or I, a default_tax_code a create rejects, or a name with spaces at either end. Such a group matches neither status=A nor status=I, so those two counts can add up to less than the total.
  • After a group delete returns 404, your next customer delete can fail. The next Delete a customer call on the same connection can return 404 with the message Customer already in trash, or 204, and not delete the customer: its is_in_trash stays false. Send that customer delete on a new connection, or send it again.
